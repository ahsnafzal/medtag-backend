Why “API Call Log” can stay empty: JazzCash usually shows a finalized row only after (1) Pay Now POSTs from this page to JazzCash, (2) you complete payment on their hosted page, and (3) JazzCash POSTs the result to your Return URL. Missing any step often means “no completed transaction yet”—finish a full sandbox payment first, wait for redirect back here, then refresh the merchant dashboard.
